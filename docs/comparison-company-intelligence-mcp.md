## Key Differences

### AI Agent Integration
Company Intelligence MCP is built for AI agents using the MCP protocol. AI agents call tools like `company_enrich(domain="stripe.com")` directly — no CRM integration, no scraping code, no sales team.

ZoomInfo and Apollo.io are human-facing sales tools with REST APIs designed for human users. API integration requires engineering effort.

### Compliance Features
Company Intelligence MCP includes sanctions screening (OFAC SDN, OpenSanctions, Interpol Red Notices) and beneficial ownership tracing — critical for AML/KYC compliance.

Neither ZoomInfo nor Apollo.io focuses on compliance use cases.

### Pricing Model
Company Intelligence MCP: pay per call with no monthly commitment. Useful for occasional queries or high-volume AI workflows.

ZoomInfo: $99+/month for base tier, higher for advanced features
Apollo.io: $49+/month for base tier

At $0.10/call, you get 490 company enriches for the price of one Apollo.io month — without a subscription.

## When to Choose Each

**Choose Company Intelligence MCP when:**
- You're an AI agent doing due diligence, compliance screening, or lead research
- You need sanctions screening and beneficial ownership data
- You want pay-per-use without a subscription
- You don't want to go through a sales process
- You're building an AI product and need company data

**Choose ZoomInfo when:**
- Your team manually does outbound sales research
- You need a full sales engagement platform (email sequences, calling, etc.)
- You have a sales team that will use the tool directly

**Choose Apollo.io when:**
- You need email outreach automation alongside data
- Your team does manual outbound prospecting
- You want built-in email sequences and CRM features
